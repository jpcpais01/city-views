# City Views patch notes

## 2026-10-24 23:02 UTC
- Dead-end streets now finish in a rounded turning head, with the kerb and pavement carried round, instead of stopping square.
- Tapping a building picks the one you see under your finger: the tap follows the buildings' real walls and roofs instead of the plots on the ground, so a low house in front or a yard no longer gets in the way. The building you picked glows while its card is up.

## 2026-10-24 22:02 UTC
- Hyper graphics in photo mode: the camera button now renders at twice the resolution, with a shadow map four times as detailed, the softest shadow edges, ambient occlusion that darkens the corners, gaps and building feet where real skylight can't reach, finer sky reflections and a gentle glow by day. Normal play is unchanged, and Settings can turn it off.
- The camera glides over hills and valleys: it follows the lie of the land around the view instead of the spot under it, and eases up and down instead of jolting.
- Town goals now sit under your city's name.
- Brushes feel instant: the Grow and Green tint shows under your finger as you paint, the ground reshapes while you drag instead of after, picking the Grow brush no longer redraws the whole map, and background work (new houses, woods, yards, streets) shares one budget per frame so painting stays smooth.
- Avenues and highways smooth out a shaky hand as you draw them: avenues follow your line in easy curves, highways in long, sweeping ones. Streets still go exactly where you draw.

## 2026-10-24 21:11 UTC
- A new lighting pass: shadows reach right across the view and stay crisp at every zoom, the haze no longer washes the city out, a lower sun throws shadows you can read, colours look like film, the sky glows round a low sun and nights are deeper and cooler.
- Town goals sit in a small tidy panel beside your city's name: one line per goal with a thin progress bar, ready ones first, and it folds away from its header.
- Yards lose their floor pads, so benches, trees and parked cars stand on the grass, and dense districts are paved with stone slabs between their streets and buildings while the suburbs keep their lawns.
- The open sea no longer shows tiled ripples or pale blotches, and every boat is far more detailed, each leaving a foaming wake: yachts with full sails and rigging, fishing boats with outriggers and nets, and cruise ships, container ships and ferries with lifeboats, cranes, radar masts and bridge wings. The airliner is rebuilt too, with a round fuselage, engines, winglets and its airline's livery.
- Tilt the camera right up to look across your city from the side, almost at street level.

## 2026-10-24 20:07 UTC
- Bus stops: main streets and avenues get glass shelters with a bench and an advert that lights up at night, and buses now keep to the kerb lane and pull in at each stop.
- Zoom out twice as far to take in your whole city and the country around it, and on PC tilt the camera up towards the horizon much further (right mouse button, slide up or down).
- A Check for updates button in Settings shows which version you have and looks for a new one any time.

## 2026-10-24 19:00 UTC
- Undo and Redo are instant: they only put back what your last stroke changed instead of reloading the whole city, and taking up a street is much quicker too.
- Time of day in Settings (Auto, Sunrise, Day, Sunset or Night), and on PC hold the right mouse button and slide up or down to tilt the camera.
- Every district keeps its own look all the way up: homes grow into balconied residential towers with roof gardens, only business districts grow offices, and shopping districts grow department stores, cinemas and glass-domed malls, then a Ferris wheel and a floodlit stadium.
- Bridges are real bridges now: stone arches with cutwater piers over short crossings, and over longer ones a steel arch, a cable-stayed span from white pylons or a suspension bridge with tall towers, all with railings, lamps and floodlights at night.
- No more stutters while your city builds: landmarks, gardens, the ground, farmland, shadows, new streets and autosaves now do their work a little at a time instead of all in one frame.

## 2026-10-24 18:10 UTC

- Airliners now cross high over your city every minute or so, with glowing cabin windows and red and green wingtip lights at night.

## 2026-10-24 16:03 UTC

- Unlocks: Shops and Industry districts open at 60 and 120 residents, Offices at 300, the Downtown level at 600 and Skyline at 1,200. Bigger cities you already have keep everything they've reached.
- City stats: tap your population for a panel with your milestone and what the next one opens, residents, jobs and how many are out of work, homes, shops, works, streets, traffic and taxes.

## 2026-10-24 15:40 UTC

## 2026-10-24 14:45 UTC
